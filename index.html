CliniGuard AI

An AI-powered clinical trial risk monitoring concept designed to detect protocol deviations early and help clinical teams take corrective action.

Problem

Clinical trials involve large numbers of patient visits across multiple sites. Missed visits, incorrect dosing and prohibited medications can remain hidden until late-stage review or audit.

Our Solution

CliniGuard compares trial records with protocol rules, detects deviations, classifies their severity and converts these signals into site-level risk insights.
